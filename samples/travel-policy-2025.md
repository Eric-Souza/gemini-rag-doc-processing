# Acme Travel & Expense Policy, 2025 Edition (Sample)

This policy applies to business travel booked between January 1, 2025 and December 31, 2025. It is superseded by the 2026 edition for travel booked on or after January 1, 2026.

## Booking
All flights and hotels must be booked through the Navan travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed unless the portal had no availability, and a screenshot is attached to the expense report.

## Flights
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class requires VP approval.

## Hotels
- Nightly hotel cap: $200 in standard cities and $275 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo).
- Stays over 7 consecutive nights require manager approval.

## Meals and per diem
- Daily meal per diem: $65 for domestic travel and $80 for international travel.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Ground transportation
Rideshare and taxis are reimbursed for business trips. Car rentals require manager approval and must be mid-size or smaller. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.

## Expense reports
Submit expense reports within 30 days of returning from a trip. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
