Project Nova — Internal Product Brief
=====================================

Codename: Nova
Owner: Product Ops
Status: Pilot
Launch target: September 15, 2026

Problem
-------
Support agents spend too long searching across PDFs and wiki pages to answer
customer questions. Average handle time for policy questions is 7.4 minutes.

Solution
--------
Nova is an internal document Q&A assistant. Agents upload policy PDFs, then
ask natural-language questions. Answers must include source citations.

Success metrics
---------------
1. Reduce policy-related handle time to under 3 minutes.
2. Citation accuracy above 90% in weekly spot audits.
3. At least 80% of Tier-1 agents use Nova weekly within 60 days of launch.

Pilot scope
-----------
- Teams: Support Tier-1 (North America)
- Document types: benefits handbook, refund policy, shipping SLA
- Model: Google Gemini flash for chat; Gemini embedding model for retrieval

Known constraints
-----------------
- Free-tier API quotas apply during the pilot.
- No customer PII should be uploaded into the pilot corpus.
- Chat history is not persisted server-side in v1.
