# Acme Information Security Policy (Sample)

## Accounts and passwords
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for all accounts. Hardware security keys are required for administrators and anyone with production access.

## Devices
- Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Personal phones may access email and chat only if enrolled in device management.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 1 hour.

## Data handling
- Customer data is classified as Confidential and may only be stored in approved systems.
- Confidential data must not be pasted into public AI tools. Use the approved internal assistant instead.
- Production database exports require a ticket approved by the data owner and expire after 7 days.

## Security incidents
Report suspected security incidents to security@acme.example within 24 hours of discovery. Phishing emails should be reported with the "Report Phish" button and then deleted.

## Access reviews
Managers review their team's access every quarter. Access for departing employees is revoked on their last working day.
