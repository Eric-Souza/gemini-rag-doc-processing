# Refund & Shipping Policy (Sample)

## Refunds
Customers may request a refund within **30 days** of delivery for unused items
in original packaging. Digital goods are non-refundable after download.

Refund method:
- Credit card purchases: refunded to the original card within 5-7 business days
- Store credit: issued instantly and expires after 12 months

## Exchanges
Size/color exchanges are free for standard ground shipping within the US.
International exchanges are not supported; customers should request a refund
and place a new order.

## Shipping SLA
| Method | Domestic ETA | Cost |
|--------|--------------|------|
| Standard | 5-7 business days | Free over $50 |
| Express | 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $29.99 |

Orders placed after 2:00 PM local warehouse time ship the next business day.

## Damaged items
Report damage within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Acme will ship a
replacement at no cost or issue a full refund.
