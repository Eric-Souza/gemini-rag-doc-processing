# Acme Travel & Expense Policy, 2026 Edition (Sample)

This policy applies to business travel booked on or after January 1, 2026. It replaces the 2025 edition.

## What changed in 2026
- Higher hotel caps and meal per diems.
- Expense reports are now due within 21 days instead of 30.
- Rail is now required for trips under 4 hours where a train route exists.

## Booking
All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the Navan travel portal. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed unless the portal had no availability, and a screenshot is attached to the expense report.

## Flights and rail
- Rail is required for trips under 4 hours where a direct train route exists.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Business class requires VP approval.

## Hotels
- Nightly hotel cap: $230 in standard cities and $320 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich).
- Stays over 10 consecutive nights require manager approval.

## Meals and per diem
- Daily meal per diem: $75 for domestic travel and $95 for international travel.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Ground transportation
Rideshare and taxis are reimbursed for business trips. Car rentals require manager approval and must be mid-size or smaller. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at $0.70 per mile.

## Expense reports
Submit expense reports within 21 days of returning from a trip. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.
