# Nimbus Audio Product Catalog (Sample)

## Headsets

### NV-220 Wired Headset
Entry-level wired USB headset for call centers. Noise-cancelling boom microphone, 3.5 mm and USB-A connectors. Price: $49. Warranty: 1 year.

### NV-220X Wireless Headset
Wireless version of the NV-220 with Bluetooth 5.3 and a USB-C dongle. Battery life: 28 hours of talk time. Price: $129. Warranty: 2 years.

### NV-480 Pro Headset
Premium wireless headset with active noise cancellation and a mute indicator light. Battery life: 40 hours of talk time. Price: $219. Warranty: 3 years, including one accidental damage replacement.

## Speakerphones

### SP-10 Speakerphone
Portable USB speakerphone for rooms of up to 4 people. Pickup range: 2 meters. Price: $99. Warranty: 1 year.

### SP-30 Conference Speakerphone
Conference speakerphone for rooms of up to 12 people, daisy-chainable to a second SP-30. Pickup range: 5 meters. Price: $349. Warranty: 2 years.

## Support
Firmware updates are installed through the Nimbus Hub desktop app. Warranty claims require the serial number printed inside the left ear cup (headsets) or on the base (speakerphones). Replacement units ship within 3 business days of an approved claim.
