# Acme Benefits Handbook (Sample)

## Overview
Acme Corp offers health, dental, and wellness benefits to full-time employees.
Eligibility begins on the first day of the month after 30 days of employment.

## Health Plan
- Plan name: Acme Care Plus
- Deductible: $1,000 individual / $2,000 family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $4,500 individual / $9,000 family
- Primary care visit copay: $25
- Specialist visit copay: $50
- Emergency room: $250 (waived if admitted)

## Dental Plan
- Preventive care covered at 100%
- Basic procedures covered at 80% after deductible
- Annual maximum benefit: $1,500 per person

## Wellness Stipend
Employees receive a $600 annual wellness stipend for gym memberships,
fitness classes, or mental health apps. Requests must be submitted through
Workday by December 1 each year.

## PTO Policy
- Years 0-2: 15 days PTO
- Years 3-5: 20 days PTO
- Years 6+: 25 days PTO
Unused PTO may roll over up to 5 days into the next calendar year.
